# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Smart capture: drag an area, click a window, or click open space for the
  whole screen. Area, Window, Screen and Scroll modes.
- Floating previews that stack, fan out on hover, and pin on screen.
- An annotation editor with arrows, lines, pen, highlighter, shapes, numbered
  markers, text, pixel-destroying redaction, spotlight, cut, OCR, eyedropper,
  backdrops, and unlimited undo.
- Scroll capture that stitches a scrolling area into one image.
- Recent captures that reopen with their layers still editable.
- Configurable hotkeys, a `macsnap` command and a `macsnap://` URL scheme.
- A welcome window that walks through Screen Recording permission.
